## Refund eligibility

Before 1 August 2026, customers could request a refund for a damaged physical
item within 21 calendar days of delivery. Photo evidence was required before a
damaged-item refund could be approved.

## Refund approval limits

Before 1 August 2026, refunds up to and including USD 75.00 could be
automatically approved after eligibility and evidence requirements were met.
Refunds from USD 75.01 through USD 300.00 required human approval. Refunds above
USD 300.00 required human takeover.
