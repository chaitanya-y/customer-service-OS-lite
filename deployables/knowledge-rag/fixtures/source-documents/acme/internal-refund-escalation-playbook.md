# Acme Internal Refund Escalation Playbook

**Document status:** Synthetic learning fixture  
**Classification:** INTERNAL_ONLY  
**Audience:** Acme customer-support operations staff  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-01

## Purpose

This playbook helps an authorized support agent decide when a refund case must be
escalated. It is not customer-facing policy and must never be included in a
customer response or customer retrieval result.

## Escalate immediately

Create a human-takeover case when any of these conditions applies:

- The requested refund is above USD 500.00.
- The customer disputes a charge and threatens legal action.
- Order facts conflict across the commerce system and CRM.
- The customer reports repeated delivery loss or suspected fraud.
- The request concerns an exception not covered by the published refund policy.

## Approval review

For a refund above USD 100.00 and up to USD 500.00, collect the verified order
facts and route the case to an authorized approver. Do not tell the customer that
approval is guaranteed.

## Handoff notes

The handoff record must include the order ID, customer verification result,
requested amount, policy evidence, tool results, and the reason for escalation.
